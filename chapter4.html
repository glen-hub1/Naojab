Chapter 4: Financial Struggles

One of the biggest challenges facing the new generation is financial instability. Many young people struggle with unemployment, underemployment, low income, rising costs of living, debt, and the pressure to maintain a lifestyle they cannot afford.

In many countries, graduates complete their education only to discover that jobs are scarce. Others find work, but the income is not enough to cover housing, food, transport, healthcare, and family responsibilities.

Common Financial Challenges

  • Unemployment
  • Low income
  • Student debt
  • Poor budgeting habits
  • Impulse buying
  • Peer pressure spending
  • Dependence on loans and credit

For example, a university student may receive money for food and rent but spend a large portion on entertainment, expensive clothing, or unnecessary online purchases. By the end of the month, they are forced to borrow money from friends or family.

Consequences of Financial Struggles

  • Stress and anxiety
  • Depression
  • Family conflict
  • Relationship problems
  • Delayed education or career goals
  • Poor health due to inadequate nutrition
  • Loss of confidence and motivation
“Money is a tool. When managed wisely, it creates opportunities; when managed poorly, it creates chains.”

Solutions

  • Create a monthly budget
  • Track every expense
  • Save a small amount consistently
  • Avoid unnecessary debt
  • Learn financial literacy and investing
  • Develop income-generating skills
  • Start small businesses or freelance work when possible
  • Live below your means

Financial freedom is rarely achieved overnight. It is built through discipline, patience, hard work, and wise decision-making over time.
