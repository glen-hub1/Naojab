Chapter 6: Identity, Self-Worth, and Purpose

One of the greatest struggles of the new generation is the search for identity and purpose. Many young people constantly ask themselves: Who am I? Why am I here? What is my purpose in life?

In a world filled with comparison, social media influence, and external expectations, many individuals begin to measure their value by their appearance, money, popularity, relationships, or academic achievements. When these things disappear, they feel empty and worthless.

Common Identity Struggles

  • Low self-esteem
  • Feeling not good enough
  • Comparing yourself with others
  • Seeking validation from social media
  • Fear of failure
  • Lack of direction in life
  • Confusion about career and purpose

A student may believe that because another classmate is wealthier, more attractive, or more successful, they are less valuable. This belief slowly destroys confidence and motivation.

Effects of Poor Self-Worth

  • Depression and anxiety
  • People-pleasing behavior
  • Toxic relationships
  • Poor decision-making
  • Fear of taking opportunities
  • Loss of ambition
  • Dependence on others for happiness
“Your value does not come from people’s opinions. It comes from who you are and the purpose you choose to pursue.”

How to Build Self-Worth and Purpose

  • Know your strengths and talents
  • Stop comparing your journey with others
  • Set meaningful personal goals
  • Develop skills that create value
  • Spend time with positive and supportive people
  • Read books that inspire growth
  • Pray, reflect, and seek spiritual guidance
  • Serve others through your gifts and abilities

Purpose is discovered through growth, service, discipline, and continuous learning. The most fulfilled people are not always the richest; they are often those who know why they are living.
